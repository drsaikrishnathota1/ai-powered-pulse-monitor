# App Review Response - Guideline 5.2.5

Submission ID: 1213b1cd-6647-4c33-89f5-292c034f63fa
Review date: May 05, 2026
Version reviewed: 1.0 (1)
App: Camvital Scan

## Issue

Apple reported that the App Store metadata used the term `iPhone` in the subtitle in a way that may cause confusion with Apple products or services.

## Corrected Metadata

Use this App Store Connect subtitle:

`Camera pulse and wellness tracker`

Avoid these subtitle patterns:

- `Pulse monitor for iPhone`
- `Heart rate scanner for iPhone`
- `iPhone pulse and wellness tracker`
- Any subtitle that includes `iPhone`, `iPad`, `Apple`, `App Store`, `Siri`, `HealthKit`, or other Apple product/service names.

## Reply To Apple

Hello App Review,

Thank you for the review. We removed the Apple product term from the Camvital Scan app subtitle and updated the subtitle to:

`Camera pulse and wellness tracker`

The metadata no longer includes Apple product or service terms in a confusing or inappropriate manner.

Thank you.
