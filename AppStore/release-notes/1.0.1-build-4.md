# Camvital Scan 1.0.1 Build 4 Release Notes

This update improves the reliability of one-minute camera pulse scans.

- Improved LED flash handling during guided scans.
- Added a watchdog that attempts to keep the torch active during the measurement session.
- Stabilized camera capture timing for more consistent signal processing.
- Improved pulse estimation validation so weak or noisy signals are rejected instead of saved as readings.
- Increased final signal-quality threshold before saving a BPM result.
- Improved low-signal guidance for users covering the camera and LED.

Camvital Scan remains a wellness app and is not a medical device or emergency monitor.
