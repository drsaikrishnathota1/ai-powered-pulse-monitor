## Description
Camvital Scan helps you estimate pulse and understand wellness trends using camera-based optical sensing.

Hold your fingertip gently over the rear camera and light, complete a short measurement, and review your estimated BPM, signal quality, pulse zone, and recent history.

Features:
- Camera-based pulse estimation
- Live optical waveform
- Signal quality guidance
- Pulse zones by age
- Local reading history
- Optional health data export
- Privacy-focused design with no account required

Camvital Scan is intended for general wellness and fitness awareness only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. For medical concerns, consult a qualified clinician.
